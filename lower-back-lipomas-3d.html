Anatomical Landmarks & Plane
Both nodules reside within the subcutaneous fat layer of the lower lumbar paramedian gutters, directly superficial to the posterior sheet of the thoracolumbar fascia. They sit approximately at the level of the L5 spinous process and L5–S1 disc, approximately 4.6 cm (right) and 5.2 cm (left) lateral to the posterior midline.
Left Lipoma Interval Growth Analysis
Comparing the Jul 22, 2025 and May 14, 2026 ultrasound studies reveals significant morphological interval thickening of the left lesion:
  • Thickness (depth) increased from 9 mm to 23 mm (+155% increase).
  • Axial cross-sectional area increased from 2.76 cm² to 5.78 cm² (+109% increase).
  • Estimated volume expanded from ~3.68 cm³ to ~7.71 cm³ (~+110% growth).
  • Solid, isoechoic to adjacent subcutaneous adipose tissue, benign sonographic architecture without cystic degeneration.
Spine Bone & Disc Findings
Radiographs and MRI confirm preserved spinal column alignment, mild L5–S1 disc space narrowing (represented in the 3D model with reduced disc height), and mild facet arthrosis at bilateral L4–L5 and right L5–S1. Sacroiliac joints are anatomically intact.
